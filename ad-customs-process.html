Import Process

From purchase in Japan to arrival in Papua New Guinea — the complete process explained.

Section 3

Japan Vehicle Purchase Process

Key steps to successfully purchase a used vehicle from Japan

3.1

Choose a Trusted Sales Agent

  • Work with reputable dealers / auction houses in Japan
  • Get full vehicle details and clear photos
  • Confirm purchase price, shipping terms and delivery time
3.2

Vehicle Verification

  • Check auction sheet and grade
  • Verify chassis / frame number (no tampering)
  • Confirm mileage and condition
  • Ensure no outstanding loans or liens
3.3

Documents from Japan

  • Sales invoice / proforma invoice
  • Auction sheet
  • Export certificate (deregistration)
  • Bill of Lading (after shipment)
  • Original vehicle documents (for RTA)

Section 5

Shipping & Freight Forwarding

Getting your vehicle safely from Japan to Papua New Guinea

🚢 Shipping Methods
  • RoRo (Roll-on/Roll-off) — most common for vehicles
  • Container — used for special vehicles or mixed cargo
📄 Key Shipping Documents
  • Bill of Lading (B/L)
  • Packing list
  • Commercial invoice
  • Certificate of origin
⏱️ Transit Time (Approx.)
  • Japan to PNG: 3 – 5 weeks
  • Varies by port & vessel schedule
  • Ensure shipping line and forwarder provide tracking and arrival schedule updates
