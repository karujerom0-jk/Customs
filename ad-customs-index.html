How It Works

Used Vehicle Import Overview

PNG imports used vehicles mainly from Japan, Thailand, Australia, New Zealand, UK, USA and other countries. Here is the complete process.

01
🛒

Vehicle Purchase & Verification

Purchase and thorough verification of the vehicle in the country of origin (mainly Japan).

02
🚢

Export & Shipping

Export procedures, shipping arrangement (RoRo or Container) and freight forwarding to PNG.

03
📋

Customs Clearance (PNG)

Lodgement of import declaration, classification, valuation, duty calculation and payment.

04
🔬

NAQIA / Biosecurity

Biosecurity permit application, inspection and clearance certificate before release into PNG.

05
🏗️

Port & Terminal Procedures

Wharf procedures, document submission, payment of charges and vehicle inspection.

06
🚛

Land Transport & Registration

Delivery to inland destination and RTA inspection & registration to put the vehicle on the road.
