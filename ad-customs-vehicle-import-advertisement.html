A clear process

Know the steps before your vehicle ships.

Every import can differ depending on the vehicle, country of export, shipping arrangement and current PNG requirements. Use this overview to prepare questions and organise your documents.

  • Choose a reputable seller and verify vehicle details.
  • Confirm eligibility, import conditions and required permits before shipping.
  • Collect export, purchase and shipping documents.
  • Coordinate arrival, customs assessment and biosecurity checks.
  • Arrange release and inland delivery after applicable requirements are met.
Importer checklist

Prepare early. Avoid preventable delays.

Keep copies of your purchase invoice, vehicle identification details, export certificate, bill of lading and any permits or supporting documents requested by the relevant authorities or your customs broker.

Important: Duties, taxes, eligibility rules, permits and inspection requirements can change. Confirm the current position with the appropriate PNG authorities and your licensed customs broker.

Get organised

Common documents to prepare.

The exact list depends on your vehicle and circumstances. Ask for a case-specific checklist before dispatch.

🧾

Purchase & Value

Commercial invoice or sale invoice, purchase details and information supporting the declared value.

🚢

Shipping Records

Bill of lading, shipping or packing information, and details of the exporter, importer and destination.

🚗

Vehicle Records

Chassis or VIN details, make, model, year, engine information and export or deregistration certificate where applicable.

🌏

Origin & Insurance

Certificate of origin or insurance documentation if applicable or requested for your shipment.

📝

Permits & Declarations

Any required importer registration, permits, declarations or other supporting records confirmed for your case.

📁

Keep Copies

Store clear digital and printed copies, and ensure names, vehicle identifiers and shipment details match.
