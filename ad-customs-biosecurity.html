NAQIA / Biosecurity Procedures

Used vehicles must be cleared by NAQIA (or the current authority – Biosecurity PNG) before release into PNG

1

Biosecurity permit (before arrival)

2

Submit Details

Vehicle details and documents

4

Clearance

Clearance certificate issued

⚠️
Important

Requirements may change. Always confirm the current authority and procedures with Biosecurity PNG. Proper cleaning of the vehicle before export significantly reduces inspection issues.

Section 11

Port & Terminal Procedures

What happens at the port once your vehicle arrives

🏗️ Wharf Procedures
  • Submit shipping documents
  • Pay wharfage / handling charges
  • Vehicle inspection and documentation
📦 Terminal Storage
  • Vehicles may be stored at the terminal or customs wharf
  • Be aware of the 30-day rule
🚨
Critical Warning – 30-Day Rule

If a vehicle remains at the Customs wharf for more than 30 days, it can be forfeited under PNG Customs regulations. Arrange clearance and collection promptly.
