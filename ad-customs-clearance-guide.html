Used Vehicle Import Overview

PNG imports used vehicles mainly from Japan, Thailand, Australia, New Zealand, UK, USA and other countries.

01
🛒

Vehicle Purchase & Verification

Purchase and thorough verification of the vehicle in the country of origin (mainly Japan).

02
🚢

Export & Shipping

Export procedures, shipping arrangement (RoRo or Container) and freight forwarding to PNG.

03
📋

Customs Clearance (PNG)

Lodgement of import declaration, classification, valuation, duty calculation and payment.

04
🔬

NAQIA / Biosecurity

Biosecurity permit application, inspection and clearance certificate before release into PNG.

05
🏗️

Port & Terminal Procedures

Wharf procedures, document submission, payment of charges and vehicle inspection.

06
🚛

Land Transport & Registration

Delivery to inland destination and RTA inspection & registration to put the vehicle on the road.

ℹ️
Important Note

Used vehicles are subject to PNG Customs classification, valuation, duties, excise and GST. Vehicles must also meet biosecurity and safety requirements before release.

Section 3

Japan Vehicle Purchase Process

Key steps to successfully purchase a used vehicle from Japan

3.1

Choose a Trusted Sales Agent

  • Work with reputable dealers / auction houses in Japan
  • Get full vehicle details and clear photos
  • Confirm purchase price, shipping terms and delivery time
3.2

Vehicle Verification

  • Check auction sheet and grade
  • Verify chassis / frame number (no tampering)
  • Confirm mileage and condition
  • Ensure no outstanding loans or liens
3.3

Documents from Japan

  • Sales invoice / proforma invoice
  • Auction sheet
  • Export certificate (deregistration)
  • Bill of Lading (after shipment)
  • Original vehicle documents (for RTA)

Section 5

Shipping & Freight Forwarding

Getting your vehicle safely from Japan to Papua New Guinea

🚢 Shipping Methods
  • RoRo (Roll-on/Roll-off) — most common for vehicles
  • Container — used for special vehicles or mixed cargo
📄 Key Shipping Documents
  • Bill of Lading (B/L)
  • Packing list
  • Commercial invoice
  • Certificate of origin
⏱️ Transit Time (Approx.)
  • Japan to PNG: 3 – 5 weeks
  • Varies by port & vessel schedule
  • Ensure shipping line and forwarder provide tracking and arrival schedule updates

Section 7

PNG Importer Requirements

What you need as an importer before the shipment arrives in PNG

Importer Must Have

  • ✓ Be registered with PNG Customs (TIN)
  • ✓ Obtain a G26 import permit (if required)
  • ✓ Have a valid business licence
  • ✓ Appoint a licensed Customs broker (or use AD Customs)
  • ✓ Provide a suitable address and contact details

💡 Pro Tip

Ensure your TIN and G26 are up to date before the shipment arrives in PNG. Delays in documentation can result in storage charges and demurrage.

AD Customs can assist with broker services and guide you through every requirement.

Section 9

Vehicle Classification & Valuation

Correct classification is vital for accurate duties, excise and GST

HS Classification

Used motor vehicles (e.g. 8703) are classified under HS 8703. Correct classification is vital for accurate duties, excise and GST calculation.

  • ● Based on CIF value (Cost + Insurance + Freight)
  • ● Use the transaction value or alternative methods (as per WTO rules)
  • ● Ensure all costs are properly declared
Standard HS Code
8703
Used Motor Vehicles

Section 11

Port & Terminal Procedures

What happens at the port once your vehicle arrives

🏗️ Wharf Procedures
  • Submit shipping documents
  • Pay wharfage / handling charges
  • Vehicle inspection and documentation
📦 Terminal Storage
  • Vehicles may be stored at the terminal or customs wharf
  • Be aware of the 30-day rule
🚨
Critical Warning – 30-Day Rule

If a vehicle remains at the Customs wharf for more than 30 days, it can be forfeited under PNG Customs regulations. Arrange clearance and collection promptly.
