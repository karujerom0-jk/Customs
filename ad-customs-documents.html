Documents Required

Essential paperwork for a smooth customs clearance process from Japan to Papua New Guinea.

Section 6

Documents Required (Japan to PNG)

Make sure you have all the following documents ready before the shipment arrives.

📑 Core Shipping Documents

  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Bill of Lading (B/L)
  • Auction Sheet / Vehicle Details
  • Export Certificate (Deregistration)

🔍 Verification & Supporting

  • Chassis Number Verification
  • Certificate of Origin (if required)
  • Insurance Certificate
  • Any other supporting documents (as advised)
Section 7

PNG Importer Requirements

What you need as an importer before the shipment arrives in PNG

Importer Must Have

  • ✓ Be registered with PNG Customs (TIN)
  • ✓ Obtain a G26 import permit (if required)
  • ✓ Have a valid business licence
  • ✓ Appoint a licensed Customs broker (or use AD Customs)
  • ✓ Provide a suitable address and contact details

💡 Important Tip

Ensure your TIN and G26 are up to date before the shipment arrives in PNG.

Delays in documentation can result in storage charges and demurrage. AD Customs can assist with broker services and guide you through every requirement.

Need Help with Documentation?

AD Customs can review your documents in advance, advise on any missing items, and lodge the import declaration on your behalf. Contact us early to avoid delays.
